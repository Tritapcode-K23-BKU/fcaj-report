## Reflection on "Agentic AI Build Week & Hackathon Journey"

**Purpose of the event:**
- Experience a continuous 24-hour cycle of ideating and perfecting Agentic AI products.
- Listen to outstanding practical AI solutions from competing teams.
- Develop resilience to overcome technical issues and manage time.

**List of speakers:**
- Mr. Joseph Marazota (Head of Technology for ASEAN, AWS)
- Hackathon teams: One Team, Signal Scout, Team Plan, 3K, Six Pillar

### Key Highlights

**1. Opening Speech (Joseph Marazota)**

Shared a vision of AI reshaping industries, and the critical role of humans in the loop — the "human in the loop" principle.

**2. KFC Food Ordering Project (1st Place — One Team)**

A Zalo AI assistant built with Tiny Fish and Agent Core to remember conversation context, delivering ultra-low latency and optimized operating costs.

**3. Strategy Analysis Project (2nd Place — Signal Scout)**

Gathering competitor signals using multiple Subagents, combined with Bedrock Guardrail and LlamaIndex to reduce AI hallucinations.

**4. Solution Architect Assistant (Team Plan)**

Automatically drawing Draw.io diagrams and generating Terraform code directly from natural language requirements.

**5. Crowd Management (Team 3K — Sheper) & Anti-Money Laundering (Six Pillar)**

Coordinating Kinesis, YOLO, and AgentCore for real-time video stream processing; an automated fraud analysis system across 3 layers (XGBoost, Step Functions, Agent).

### Results Achieved

- Mastered how to combine Agentic AI models with AWS cloud infrastructure in a real-world project.
- Internalized the "Done is better than perfect" philosophy when working under the high time pressure of a hackathon.

### Applications to Work

1. Apply Agentic AI in personal projects to automate action chains.
2. Better manage project scope in future teamwork.

### Experience at the Event

An emotional rollercoaster — debugging overnight until morning, then bursting with joy upon a successful demo.
