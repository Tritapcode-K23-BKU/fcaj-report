#### Week 12 — Next steps

**Dates:** After the internship

#### Goals

- Identify knowledge gaps to close
- Plan certification study
- Build a personal project to consolidate learning

#### Work carried out

Reviewed my weak areas after the internship. AWS networking was barely touched
because the project architecture was serverless, making it my largest gap. I also
have no practical container experience, which is necessary for deploying heavier
machine learning models to the cloud.

Planned study for the AWS Solutions Architect Associate certification, since its
syllabus covers exactly the areas I am missing.

Decided to rebuild a small project from scratch without following any guide, to
test how well I actually understand the material.

#### Results

- A clear list of knowledge gaps to close
- A concrete study plan for the next phase
- An idea for the next personal project

#### Difficulties and how they were resolved

The hardest thing is distinguishing between feeling that you understand and
actually understanding. Writing the workshop documentation revealed several
places where I had only been following along. That is why I chose to rebuild a
project from scratch rather than study more theory.
